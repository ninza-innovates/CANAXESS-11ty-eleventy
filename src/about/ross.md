## Ross Mullen
**Ross is director of CANAXESS**, and is passionate about making web accessibility easier to understand and implement. 

He believes accessibility should be simple and straightforward for teams, which led him to create <a href="https://www.thisiswcag.com">thisiswcag.com</a>, a community resource designed to clear up confusion and help people use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) effectively.

With over 20 years of experience in web development and software engineering, including 15 years with the Australian government. Ross has spent much of his career working on web accessibility. He’s written hundreds of audit reports and collaborated with diverse teams to help their website and applications meet WCAG.  

He takes a down to earth approach, understanding the challenges of juggling tools, budgets, and deadlines.
